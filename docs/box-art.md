# NOCturne™ v1.0
### *Alert noise you can dance to.*

**THE NIGHT SHIFT WILL NEVER SOUND THE SAME.** Your infrastructure has been trying to tell you something all year. Now it can sing it. NOCturne transforms your live alert stream into an ever-evolving synth track, composed in real time by the very machines that are ruining your evening.

**FEATURES:**

- **LIVE GENERATIVE SOUNDTRACK** — Every active alert becomes a note. No samples. No MP3s. 100% pure synthesis, born in your browser!
- **SEVERITY ORCHESTRATION** — Criticals growl. Warnings shimmer. You'll know how bad it is with your eyes closed!
- **EVERY DEVICE GETS ITS OWN NOTE** — That flapping interface? You'll recognize its voice across the room.
- **FEEL THE PRESSURE** — Note density tracks alert volume. Quiet NOC? Ambient bliss. Major incident? A track with *urgency*.
- **FOUR WAYS TO WATCH** — Chips for the readers. The HIVE honeycomb for the operators. CLUSTER constellations for the dreamers. And SKYLINE: your incident as a city at night, where the noisiest device is the tallest tower!
- **STORM BUTTON** — Drop a synthetic alert storm ON THE DOWNBEAT. Impress your friends! Alarm your manager!
- **AUTO-DJ** — Sit back as NOCturne rides the filters itself. Watch the sliders move LIKE A GHOST IS MIXING!
- **3 MOODS** — Minor for melancholy. Major for optimism. Dark for incidents with an audience.
- **HUSH™** — Mute the alerts, keep the groove. Perfect for pretending everything is fine!
- **RECORD YOUR HITS** — One-click capture straight to disk. The night shift's first mixtape!
- **FULL STUDIO ACCESS** — Tempo, tone, reverb, and a complete settings rack with copy/paste configs. Tweak EVERYTHING.
